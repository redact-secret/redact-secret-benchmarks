# Derive a candidate view's overlays from its own snapshot into a receipted directory, and render adoption reports from the record's current state

## Context

#699, #700, part of #690. The official replay 37202404479 of `snapshot-2026.10.04.3` ran every scanner and archived the artifacts, but its qualification-view job failed: the axis overlay still named the previous corpus (`sha256:1bc5a07b...`) and the artifact ran `sha256:fdce9df...`. The adapter's refusal was correct; the workflow supplied no coherent inputs, and the view was rebuilt by hand. The three snapshot-derived product inputs (axis overlay, twin-scope map, review-ledger re-key) are committed under `benchmarks/support/` and belong to the accepted population, so a replay of a changed snapshot could neither read them (stale) nor overwrite them (that would move the accepted state before the owner accepts). Separately, the checked-in adoption report still told the owner to accept an adoption the owner had accepted, and its introduction said decoded semantics were not measured while section 6 reported the engine mapping base64 and hex.

## Decision

1. **Derive into a directory, never into the accepted files.** `npm run qualification:derive-inputs` derives the axis overlay and twin-scope map from the verified snapshot and the review-ledger re-key from the methods artifact that ran on it, into a directory with a receipt (`derived-inputs.json`: snapshot corpus digest and bytes digest, the plain and methods artifacts' semantic digests, and one digest per generated file). It refuses an artifact of another corpus than the snapshot, and refuses `--out` under `benchmarks/`.
2. **A candidate view reads only that directory.** `npm run qualification:view -- --inputs <dir>` replaces all three committed inputs (none is read) and computes the policy revision over the derived ones. The directory is refused unless the receipt, all three files, their digests, their identity and their corpus binding hold. The adapter's own check (an input bound to another corpus than the artifact is refused with the regenerating command) is unchanged; no validator was relaxed. The committed inputs, the registry, the adoption record and the authority file change only in the owner's acceptance commit.
3. **The view stage of the official-runs workflow does this itself.** It fetches the pinned snapshot (`fetch-pinned-public-snapshot.mjs`: manifest digest, listed asset digest and size, release identity, corpus digest equal to the registry pin), derives, builds the view from the derived directory and uploads the view with the receipt. A dispatch with `reuse_run_id` skips the scanner jobs and builds the view from that earlier official-runs run's artifacts, so a view-stage failure is retried without rerunning a scanner. Verified on the archived run: the derived inputs and the view are byte-identical to the committed accepted ones.
4. **Adoption reports render from the record.** `scripts/adoption-report-state.mjs` derives the current state (adoption state, owner acceptance and its decision, replay run and archive, deployment receipts, the scanned product release) from `benchmarks/evidence-adoption.json` and the registry. Instructions written for the proposal are labelled historical once accepted; a deployment receipt is shown only if the record holds one; the maintainer-only disclosure reads "Maintainer-reviewed (independent review pending)" / "메인테이너 검토 (독립 검토 대기)" and the report says owner acceptance is not an independent evidence review; the representation wording is the engine contract's (bounded re-derivable base64 or hex mapped to the whole original encoded segment up to four layers, fragments scored on the enclosing range with no fragment-aware scoring, percent-encoded, UTF-16 and escaped-Unicode findings and unsupported mappings unmeasured); the product identity is explicit (alpha.4 measured beta.12; a beta.13 claim needs the pinned replay of the identical snapshot, #697). `--from-comparison` renders again from a recorded comparison without the views.
5. **Counts are reconciled, not assumed.** The report states the 236 changed common cases as 232 evidence-class transitions plus 4 grouping-only changes, and that 235 was the superseded candidate's figure; the generator lists a difference between the change report and the views as unexplained.

## Consequences

A changed corpus can go through replay, archive and view generation with no manual regeneration, and a failed view stage is retried from the recorded scanner artifacts. The three derived files still have to be committed by the acceptance change; the receipt makes that a review of data. A report regenerated after acceptance no longer asks for acceptance and still shows what is absent (deployment receipts, independent review). This decision settles no review decision, records no deployment and accepts nothing. Spec: `docs/specs/evidence-adoption.md`, `docs/specs/official-runs.md`.
