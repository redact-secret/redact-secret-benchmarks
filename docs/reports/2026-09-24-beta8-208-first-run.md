sibling secret.

## First run

Scanners: `@redact-secret/core` 0.1.0-beta.7 (published), gitleaks 8.30.1, TruffleHog 3.97.4 and flare-redact 1.6.1.
Command: `npm run eval -- --detector=<id>`.

An earlier attempt was preserved as-is. In that attempt gitleaks returned `error` for every case because its
`curl-auth-header` rule reports a multi-line `curl … \` command at the `curl` line, while the adapter (`locate` in
`scanners/index.mjs`) looks for the secret only on the reported start line. The Bearer header is now on the
`curl` line. The expected spans did not change. The adapter limitation still affects any multi-line curl fixture.

Canonical positives covered (the secret span is inside a finding), out of 9 per family:

| Family | redact-secret | gitleaks | TruffleHog | flare-redact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| replicate-api-token | 2 (bearer-token, generic-token) | 6 (generic-api-key, curl-auth-header) | 9 | 9 |
| groq-api-key | 2 | 8 | 9 | 9 |
| xai-api-key | 5 | 6 | 9 | 9 |
| openrouter-api-key | 3 | 8 | 9 | 9 |

- **Product:** the product has no detector for these families. It covers a positive only through the generic
  `bearer-token` or `generic-token` families (Bearer headers and some quoted `api_key`/`apiKey` assignments). Every
  env, printenv, prose, CI and JSON positive is missed. This is the expected first failing result for #727. Twin
  must-flip for redact-secret is 1/6 (Replicate), 1/6 (Groq), 4/6 (xAI) and 3/6 (OpenRouter). The twin must-not-flag
  assertions pass 6/6 for every family, because product findings on twins are generic families, which count as
  co-detection rather than false alarms for the arrival family.
- **Product false alarms on benign controls:** there are two, both from `generic-token`.
  `groq-api-key-masked-console-placeholder` has `Secret: gsk_****…Tn4q`, a masked value.
  `xai-api-key-key-metadata-public-id` has the public ACL string `"api-key:endpoint:chat"`, where the product flags
  `endpoint:chat`. Both are recorded as open ledger rows (`benign-false-alarm-unconfirmed/generic-token`).
- **Peers:** TruffleHog 3.97.4 matched 9/9 positives and 0/24 twins or controls in every family. That includes
  OpenRouter, even though `benchmarks/detector-inventory.json` lists the OpenRouter detector as feature-gated.
  flare-redact matched 9/9 positives and flagged some length twins (Replicate 2, Groq 2, xAI 4, OpenRouter 1),
  because its width ranges are looser than the contracts and its `bearer_token` rule fires on the Bearer twins.
  gitleaks has no rule for any of these families. Its findings are `generic-api-key` on `KEY=value` lines and
  `curl-auth-header`, so it also flags most structural twins (5 or 6 per family). Peer output is corroboration only
  and changed no expectation.

Saved runs: `first-run-<id>.json` (with logs), `harness-error-run/` and `final-run-<id>.json` in the #208 agent scratch.
The final run, taken after the envelope change below, gives the same per-method results as the first run.

After the first run, the envelopes on six positives were removed: the legacy `Authorization: Token` header, the xAI
`'authorization': 'Bearer …'` log line, and three JSON-key assignments. `tests/assessment.test.mjs` only admits Bearer,
URI, OTP and `name=`/`name: "` envelopes. The change came from that repository rule, not from scanner output. The
content bytes did not change.

## Not authored, and why

- Management keys and other providers' keys as controls: each is a real secret (invariant 8).
- `-`/`_` body twins (Replicate, xAI), uppercase-hex twins (OpenRouter), a 48-character Groq body, and a Groq body
  without `WGdyb3FY` used as a negative: the evidence is contested or unresolved.
- A Base64-encoded Groq key: #208 has not decided whether the encoded form is in family scope.
- Envelope for the legacy `Authorization: Token` scheme: the envelope-shape test only admits Bearer.
