# Beta.8 #210 first run: LangSmith and Langfuse

Arrival evidence for [#210](https://github.com/redact-secret/redact-secret-benchmarks/issues/210),
built from the research in [#219](https://github.com/redact-secret/redact-secret-benchmarks/issues/219)
(`langsmith:api-key`) and [#221](https://github.com/redact-secret/redact-secret-benchmarks/issues/221)
(`langfuse:secret-key`). The product counterpart is redact-secret/redact-secret#728, which is blocked by #726.
The pinned product registry (`2b98027`) has no detector for either family, so both families are
arrival families and every product miss below is the expected first result. This report records
the misses and resolves none of them.

## Contracts

Both contracts live in `benchmarks/lib/beta8/210.ts`, and each field carries its own basis.

| Arrival id | Taxonomy | Tier | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `langsmith-api-key` | `langsmith:api-key` | T2 | `^lsv2_(?:pt\|sk)_[0-9a-f]{32}_[0-9a-f]{10}$` |
| `langfuse-secret-key` | `langfuse:secret-key` | T2 | `^sk-lf-[0-9a-f]{8}-[0-9a-f]{4}-4[0-9a-f]{3}-[89ab][0-9a-f]{3}-[0-9a-f]{12}$` |

**LangSmith.** LangSmith's documentation freezes the PAT/service-key roles and the contexts. The
`lsv2_(pt|sk)_` prefix is frozen on tool evidence. The segment widths and lowercase alphabet are
tool evidence and stay provisional. The provider SDK redactor is looser (`[A-Za-z0-9]{32,}` plus
`_` tails) and is recorded as provider code. The following stay unresolved: tail semantics, legacy
`ls__`, and the sibling credentials (license key, SCIM, OAuth, `X-Service-Key` JWT, deployment
keys). None of the siblings is used as a control. The corpus has no uppercase twin.

**Langfuse.** Langfuse's documentation freezes the `sk-lf-`/`pk-lf-` prefixes, the public status
of `pk-lf-` and the contexts. The body is a lowercase UUIDv4 generated by `randomUUID()`, frozen on
provider code. The peer body rules are recorded as provisional tool evidence. The following are
recorded as unresolved and are not fixtured as controls: self-hosted operator-defined values,
`sk-lf-gw-`, and the base64 Basic-auth blob.

## First run (published 0.1.0-beta.7, gitleaks 8.30.1, trufflehog 3.97.4, flare-redact)

Canonical assertions per fixture, as passed/total:

| Family | redact-secret | gitleaks | trufflehog | flare-redact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| langsmith-api-key | 15/24 | 22/24 | 24/24 | 11/24 |
| langfuse-secret-key | 16/24 | 21/24 | 17/24 | 23/24 |

- **Product.** No positive is attributed to a LangSmith or Langfuse family.
  - LangSmith: 2 of 8 positives are covered through `generic-token` (profile JSON `api_key`,
    Python `api_key=`).
  - Langfuse: 3 of 8 positives are covered, 2 through `generic-token` and the gateway `Bearer`
    header through `bearer-token`.
  - The first failing product result is `langsmith-api-key-pat-dotenv` (must-redact T2, `.env`
    `LANGSMITH_API_KEY=`): no finding at all.
  - Every independent control and every twin variant is silent or attributed to another family.
- **Peers.**
  - trufflehog matches all 8 LangSmith positives.
  - trufflehog matches only 4 of 8 Langfuse positives. Its rule needs a `langfuse` keyword within
    40 bytes before the key and a paired `pk-lf-`, so it misses the Python and TypeScript clients,
    `curl -u` and the gateway bearer.
  - gitleaks has no rule for either family. Its `generic-api-key`, `curl-auth-header` and
    `curl-auth-user` rules catch most positives.
  - flare-redact matches no LangSmith positive and 7 of 8 Langfuse positives.
- **Peer disagreements on twins.** gitleaks flags `langsmith-api-key-short-tail-twin` through
  `curl-auth-header` and `langfuse-secret-key-variant-nibble-twin` through `curl-auth-user`. Both
  rules are unmapped, so the finding counts against the scoped twin. Its `generic-api-key` hits on
  the other twins are co-detections (`generic-token`).

## Harness notes

- `scanners/families.mjs` now maps trufflehog's `LangSmith` and `Langfuse` labels to these
  arrival ids. No existing corpus contains either prefix, so no existing ledger id moved.
- The gitleaks adapter locates a finding by its reported start line. If a match crosses a
  trailing-backslash line continuation, the finding cannot be mapped and the whole gitleaks run
  fails. The first LangSmith run hit this: gitleaks errored and recorded 0/24 in the
  preserved first-run file. The curl header fixture and one near-miss were then rewritten to a
  single line, and the LangSmith gitleaks column above comes from the re-run. Expectations did
  not change, and the product results were identical in both runs.
