# Beta.8 #259 first run: Travis CI, Neon, Postman collection access keys and the Mailgun key triplet

Evidence for [#259](https://github.com/redact-secret/redact-secret-benchmarks/issues/259), the
benchmark counterpart of redact-secret/redact-secret#773 (issues #523, #524, #700, #701). #773
merged before this evidence was authored. `benchmarks/detectors.json` is therefore pinned to
product main `3144bb3` in the same change, and three families are authored directly as registry
families, skipping the arrival stage. The published `@redact-secret/core` 0.1.0-beta.7 predates all
four shapes, so every published-mode product miss below is the expected first result. The candidate
run shows what `3144bb3` does.

## Contracts

The contracts are in `benchmarks/lib/beta8/259.ts`, and every field carries its own basis.

| Id | Kind | Taxonomy | Tier | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `travisci-api-token` | registry, context-gated | `travis-ci:api-token` | T2 | `^(?=[A-Za-z0-9]*[0-9])(?=[A-Za-z0-9]*[A-Za-z])[A-Za-z0-9]{22}$` |
| `neon-api-key` | registry | `neon:api-key` | T2 | `^napi_[A-Za-z0-9]{64,}$` |
| `postman-collection-access-key` | registry | `postman:collection-access-key` | T2 | `^PMAT-[A-Z0-9]{26}$` |
| `mailgun-api-key-triplet` | arrival, context-gated | `mailgun:legacy-signing-key-triplet` | T2 | `^[0-9a-f]{32}-[0-9a-f]{8}-[0-9a-f]{8}$` |

- **Travis CI.** The provider documents the `Authorization: token` transport and nothing else. The
  22-character length and the `travis` keyword gate come from gitleaks and trufflehog, and the
  alphabet is their intersection. The letter-and-digit requirement is the product's precision
  guard. It is recorded as a hypothesis, not a provider fact.
- **Neon.** `napi_` is provider-documented (changelog 2025-01-31). The 64-character body floor
  rests on betterleaks (exactly 64) and mask-go (64 as a floor), so the family is T2 overall.
  Legacy unprefixed keys are not claimed.
- **Postman collection access key.** Postman documents the read-only credential, and its API
  reference shows a masked `PMAT-` key 26 characters long. GitLab's rule gives `[A-Z0-9]{26}`. Its
  examples are ULID-shaped; that is recorded as unresolved and not claimed. The product also accepts
  lowercase, so no case twin is authored.
- **Mailgun key triplet.** Both pinned peers match the shape. Three sources describe it as the
  newer private API key. No issued key has been observed, so the role is recorded uncertainty.
  The taxonomy entry no longer calls it a superseded legacy signing key.

## Published mode (0.1.0-beta.7, gitleaks 8.30.1, trufflehog 3.97.4, flare-redact)

Canonical result per `beta8-259` fixture, as passed/total. A positive passes when a finding matches
the authored span exactly. A control passes when the scanner reports nothing. A twin passes when
the scanner reports nothing, or only a finding attributed to another known family.

| Family | redact-secret | gitleaks | trufflehog | flare-redact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| travisci-api-token | 16/24 | 19/24 | 22/24 | 16/24 |
| neon-api-key | 19/24 | 22/24 | 16/24 | 16/24 |
| postman-collection-access-key | 16/24 | 24/24 | 16/24 | 16/24 |
| mailgun-api-key-triplet | 38/50 | 35/50 | 41/50 | 38/50 |

- **Product.**
  - No positive is attributed to any of the four families.
  - Neon: 3 of 8 positives are covered through `bearer-token` or `generic-token`: the bearer
    header, the TypeScript `apiKey:` and the Actions `api_key:`.
  - Every control and every twin is silent.
- **Peers.**
  - gitleaks: its Travis CI rule needs an assignment operator, so it misses the CLI flag, the
    Python constructor and the log line. It covers every PMAT positive through `generic-api-key`.
    It flags 8 of the triplet's 12 context twins and 5 of its 24 controls; its keyword gate spans
    the whole line, and its alphabet is case-insensitive.
  - trufflehog: it has no Neon or PMAT rule. Its Travis CI keyword window misses the `curl`
    header. It flags 9 of the triplet's 12 context twins, because its `mailgun` keyword gate is
    chunk-wide, not line-wide.
  - flare-redact: it matches none of the four families.
