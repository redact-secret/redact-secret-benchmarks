## Measurement engines and remaining gaps

Credential-eval owns canonical measurement RunArtifacts; pii-eval owns public/synthetic PII measurement. These focused corpora and the pure harness remain explicit transitional diagnostic consumers, not a second official qualification authority. Moving code into an engine would require changing another repository and its artifact contract; it cannot be accomplished by deleting current consumers here.

The focused observation contract carries UTF-8 exact/over/under/partial/miss spans, whole versus 1/7-byte stream parity, duplicate occurrences and several runtime bindings. Neutral span/coverage calculations overlap engine measurements. Benchmark-owned expected generic type/action checks, Case provenance, controls, unsupported/conflict dispositions and support policy stay here. Concrete gaps retained without inventing engine claims: per-span must-not-flag siblings beside positive spans, policy-class may-flag tolerance, role/provider absence, endpoint/companion attribution and cross-field encoded-value consistency. D/E Cases assert no finding type/action, so their type/action pass remains false and span/coverage metrics are separate. Existing independent oracles and owner exit requirements are unchanged.
