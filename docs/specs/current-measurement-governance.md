# Current measurement governance

This is a maintained contract, not a decision log. Current domain specifications,
versioned input contracts and independently scoped authority gates govern execution.
No historical acceptance or proposed experiment grants fresh authority.

## Ownership and promotion

Benchmarks measures and records; the product owns behavioral assertions and support
claims. A measurement suggesting a new product assertion is a promotion signal.
`benchmarks/known-gaps.json` owns the observed → reviewed → promoted lifecycle.
A promoted finding needs independently authored expectation review, a canonical
product regression and a product issue or manifest receipt. Whole generated corpora,
peer outputs and private holdouts are never copied to the product. Only the core
maintainer may apply implementation-ready to a core issue. Maintainer review does
not imply independent review.

## Fixture truth and evidence levels

Provider statements bound every claimed property. T1 records exactly what the
provider documents; tool-corroborated body properties never become provider facts.
T2 provenance remains T2 when empirical corroboration clears support thresholds.
T3 is project policy, never provider format evidence. T0 is unresolved, unscored,
visible in coverage and denominators; a disputed property cannot assert silence or
redaction merely because a tool implements one interpretation.

A fixture is authored from its source contract, independently of scanner output.
A positive and its negative twin must be distinguishable by the same detector's
contract. Value twins mutate one documented property. Context twins preserve the
value byte for byte and edit only a reviewed assignment boundary. Twin scope,
family attribution, companion cardinality and pending states stay explicit.
Shared detector types map to arrival families through reviewed finding-type maps;
unknown types do not inherit the owning detector's qualification.

Provider-named credential assignments and typed neighbours are qualified project
policy. They do not become provider-format truth by relabelling. Undecided width,
alphabet, rotation and data-centre claims remain T0 history. Ory sibling contracts
remain deferred until an adopted product detector exists; registry intake alone
cannot manufacture a contract or a support claim.

Synthetic positives, twins and benign controls keep their construction provenance.
Untargeted real-world-shaped files are authored synthetic, not scraped and scrubbed
OSS. They are T3, unassigned to detector families, and cannot unblock family support.
Report warn separately from replacing actions; never relabel a noisy control to
make the measured scanner quiet. Benign evidence needs both case and distinct-axis
floors from the versioned policy, and pending axes do not count as diversity.

## Accounting and review

Per-span containment measures coverage; two findings straddling a secret do not
credit one covered secret. Partial and missed spans are leaks even if their union
covers all bytes. Collateral divides by secret bytes. Rates compare within their
own evidence level and population, with a visible denominator.

Unresolved, unstable and unmeasured cases remain visible. Review-required is not a
free pass: publish resolved/total and enforce the configured resolved-rate floor.
OVERBROAD follows the stricter assertion layer. Statistical floors and Wilson
bounds use independent units; variants, partitions and repeated checks never add
samples. Runtime and accuracy are separate measurements with separate identities.
Timing acceptance uses same-job paired ratios and the reviewed variance budget,
not an unrelated host's absolute time.

`not-assertable` is a reviewed class decision, distinct from a per-fixture resolved
review. `benchmarks/ledger-decisions.json` retains the exact accepted operator/class
mapping. Historical review queue bytes are frozen and never regenerated. A product
claim, an observation origin and a scope statement are different records; none
silently replaces another.

## Independent authority and rollback

Credential qualification and public/synthetic PII measurement each have their own
committed authority, schema, listed readers, freshness checks and owner scope.
The credential gate binds the accepted product release, policy revision, official
run digests and parity report. The PII gate binds engine, scanner package tree,
protocol, manifest and artifact digests, policy and official run. Repins invalidate
stale acceptance; fresh rehearsal and fresh owner authorisation are required.

The credential oracle exit already recorded does not retire rollback. PII remains
a bounded legacy oracle/rollback source until its independent measured exit and
owner decision hold. Protected execution and private audit remain pending and do
not gate the public lane. No agent fills owner acceptance or an owner criterion.
Historical accepted narrative is not current permission: typed provenance lists
its original status and, only for current authorisations, an exact role and target.

Official linux-x64 runs and their recorded artifacts are authoritative. Darwin
runs are local verification. The four required scanners run by default;
OpenRedaction default is positive opt-in only. Diagnostic candidate runs remain
exploratory, internal, unarchived as official runs and unpromoted. Published
results consume validated artifacts under each independent authority; the old
measurement steps remain available only for their separately governed rollback.

All historical ADR/report bytes are in the independently retrieved archive
`historical-records-872-873-v1.tar.gz` at the retention release. Source is
`40809e8ce53eb94509d3b6bb3ae877ca8d4021f4`, preserved by

`docs/retention/archive.json` records durable retrieval; a GitHub permalink is an
additional navigation aid, not the only retained copy. Restore each member and
verify its manifest length and SHA-256 before historical use. Never re-run an old
report with today's pins and call the result the original measurement.

Five archived proposals remain proposed: externally assembled intake,
peer-runtime throughput, unit-safe diagnostics, pinned Docker throughput and
independent PII evidence composition. Their full proposal text and unresolved
acceptance boundaries remain retrievable. Installed validator conformance and
protected PII execution are still pending. Removal does not accept a proposal.

New methodology belongs in a maintained specification. Proposed acceptance
packages use `benchmarks/governance/authorisations/*.json`, schema
`redact-secret/owner-authorisation/v1`, with owner fields unset. Accepted records
must state the owner, date, full ruling and exact scoped targets. Historical ADRs,
dated reports and temporary work sessions do not accumulate in HEAD.
